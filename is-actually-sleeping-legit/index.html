The guarantee, verbatim

The 14-Day Sleep Guarantee (from our Terms of Service): “We guarantee that your baby will sleep 8–12 hours through the night within 14 days of consistent, correct implementation of your sleep plan.” If that does not happen, the family receives a full refund.

  • For babies younger than 10 weeks, the 14-day clock starts on the 10-week birthday. Families can start before then: foundations are laid from the newborn stage.

Why this matters: a refund-backed outcome guarantee is rare among sleep consultants. It means the practice only makes money when the baby sleeps, which is also why it declines families it does not believe it can help.

The fair criticisms of sleep consultants — and where we stand

Parenting press has rightly pointed out that “certified sleep consultant” is not a regulated credential, that consultants may not screen for medical causes of poor sleep, and that results are rarely guaranteed. Our answers:

  • Credentials: Dr. Sam is a Doctor of Pharmacy. We do not claim a medical sleep-medicine board certification, and we say so.
