About Actually Sleeping

Actually Sleeping is a baby and toddler sleep coaching practice founded by Dr. Samantha Abrahamson (“Dr. Sam”), Founder, Doctor of Pharmacy, Sleep Consultant. The practice has coached 700+ families as of November 2026 and is headquartered at 2665 S. Bayshore Dr., Suite 220, Coconut Grove, FL 33133.

Who is Dr. Sam?

Dr. Samantha Abrahamson holds a Doctor of Pharmacy degree and founded Actually Sleeping after her own experience as the parent awake at 3 a.m. with a baby who would not sleep. She writes every client’s sleep plan personally; a coaching team runs the daily check-ins. Her husband and co-founder, Kale Abrahamson, built the tracking and coaching software the team uses.
