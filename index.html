What is Actually Sleeping?

Actually Sleeping is a 1:1 baby and toddler sleep coaching practice for families with children from newborn to 3 years. Every family gets a custom sleep plan written personally by Dr. Samantha Abrahamson (“Dr. Sam”), daily 1:1 check-ins with the coaching team, live support on Night 1, and naps coached until solid — backed by The 14-Day Sleep Guarantee. Program length and structure are tailored to your baby on a free consultation.

Actually Sleeping has coached 700+ families (as of November 2026). It is led by Dr. Samantha Abrahamson, Founder, Doctor of Pharmacy, Sleep Consultant, and based in Coconut Grove, Florida; coaching is delivered remotely over Slack and Zoom.

The 14-Day Sleep Guarantee (from our Terms of Service): “We guarantee that your baby will sleep 8–12 hours through the night within 14 days of consistent, correct implementation of your sleep plan.” If that does not happen, the family receives a full refund.

  • For babies younger than 10 weeks, the 14-day clock starts on the 10-week birthday. Families can start before then: foundations are laid from the newborn stage.
  • Night feeds are never dropped by the plan. Babies keep feeding at night for as long as they need to.
  • Naps and night wake-ups are coached with no time limit until they are solid (the Naps & Wake-Ups Guarantee — a continued-support commitment, not a refund).

How the coaching works

  1. Free consultation. A short qualification form, then a call about your baby’s sleep, schedule and history. Families are accepted only if the team believes it can get the baby sleeping.
  2. Intake and custom plan. Dr. Sam writes your plan personally — not a template or a PDF course.
  3. Night 1 with live support. A coach is with you in real time.
  4. Bedtimes, nights 1–7. Daily 1:1 check-ins in Slack; the plan is adjusted as your baby responds.
  5. Naps. The hard part, coached until solid with no time limit.
  6. Graduation. Seven sleep-throughs, then 14+ consecutive nights of 8–12 hours.

The method is not “cry it out” and not “wait it out.” It is a written plan plus daily human accountability. Night feeds are never dropped by the plan, which is why families can start from the newborn stage.

Who it is for

Parents of babies and toddlers from newborn to 3 years — commonly babies waking three to six times a night, contact nappers, babies fed or rocked to sleep, reflux babies, NICU graduates, twins, 4-month and 12-month regressions, and toddlers newly in a bed.
