Why it happens

The crib was never the problem.

1 · The cause

Sleep is a skill, not furniture.

A child who needed help falling asleep in the crib still needs it on a floor bed. The mattress changed. The skill didn't.

2 · The effect

Freedom before the skill.

Now they can't fall asleep alone and nothing keeps them in bed. So every wake-up ends with a walk to your room.

3 · What pediatricians say

There's no rush.

Most toddlers move out of the crib between 18 months and 3 years. The signs pediatricians give are being taller than 35 inches or climbing out. As one Cleveland Clinic pediatrician puts it, if a child isn't showing readiness, “there's no need to rush.” For babies under a year, the American Academy of Pediatrics says to sleep in a crib or bassinet.Sources: Cleveland Clinic, “From Crib to Toddler Bed”; American Academy of Pediatrics safe sleep recommendations (2022).
