Why does my baby wake at 5 a.m., and how do I fix it?

Early waking is almost always a schedule problem, a light problem, or a sleep-association problem — usually in that order. Check that bedtime is not too late (overtiredness causes early waking, counterintuitively), that the room is fully dark at 5 a.m., and that your baby can fall back asleep without your help. Then hold a firm “morning starts at 6” line for two weeks.

Work through the causes in order

  1. Bedtime too late. An overtired baby produces more cortisol and sleeps lighter in the early morning. Try moving bedtime 30 minutes earlier for a week. This fixes more early waking than anything else.
  2. Naps too long or too late. A late afternoon nap or too much total daytime sleep lowers sleep pressure for the end of the night. Cap the last nap so it ends by about 4 p.m.
  3. Light. The sun is up, or a hallway light is on. Early morning sleep is the lightest of the night; any light becomes a cue to wake. Blackout the room properly — test it at 5 a.m.
  4. Sleep association. The 5 a.m. waking is a cycle transition, and the baby cannot get back down without the bedtime method. Solving bedtime solves 5 a.m.
  5. Reinforced waking. If 5 a.m. means milk, a parent’s bed or screen time, it becomes the best part of the day. Keep the morning boring until the set wake time.
  6. Hunger is occasionally real in a baby under 6 months or one dropping a night feed. Offer more during the day.

The hold-the-line protocol

Choose a wake time (6 a.m. is realistic for most babies). Before that time, treat the waking exactly as you would a 2 a.m. waking: same response, no milk, no lights, no leaving the room for the day. At the set time, open the curtains, turn on lights, greet cheerfully. The contrast teaches the clock. Expect 1–2 weeks.

Frequently asked

Is 6 a.m. a normal wake time for a baby?

Yes. Most babies wake between 6 and 7 a.m. Getting a baby to sleep until 8 is rarely realistic.

Will a later bedtime make my baby sleep later?

Usually the opposite. Overtiredness causes earlier waking. Try earlier first.

Do toddler sleep-training clocks work?

They help from about 2.5–3 years, once the child understands the color change. They do not work for babies.
