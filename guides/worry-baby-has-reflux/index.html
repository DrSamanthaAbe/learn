Why parents of babies with reflux hold back

When a baby has reflux, arrived early or has a medical history, parents are understandably careful. They may be holding their baby upright for hours, or watching closely after a procedure. A generic sleep method written for an average baby does not account for any of that.

The parents in this video were in that position. What they describe is a plan written around their own baby.
