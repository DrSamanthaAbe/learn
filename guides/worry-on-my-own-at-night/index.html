Why solo nights feel impossible

When you are the only adult awake, every decision at 2 a.m. is yours. There is nobody to hand the baby to, nobody to ask whether you are doing it right, and no break in the morning. It is easy to assume sleep training needs two people.

The parents in this video were single moms, a single mom of twins, and parents whose partners worked nights, traveled or were recovering from surgery. They did it solo.
