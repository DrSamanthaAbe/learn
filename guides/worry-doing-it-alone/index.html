Will someone be with me during the night?

Coaching does not happen during the night. Instead, the bootcamp-style coaching before night one prepares you so thoroughly that you know what to do at every moment and can make decisions on your own. Daily 1:1 check-ins then review each night with you.

What happens at the daily check-ins?

You go over how the night and naps went, ask questions, and get the plan adjusted to how your baby is responding. Families describe quick responses, clear explanations and encouragement.

My partner is away a lot. Can I do this on my own?

Yes. Several parents in the reviews did most nights alone. Dr. Sam writes the plan around your household, and the coaching before night one prepares whoever is doing bedtime to make decisions with confidence.
