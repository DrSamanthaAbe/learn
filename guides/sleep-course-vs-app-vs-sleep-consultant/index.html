Sleep course vs app vs 1:1 sleep consultant: which is right for my family?

Pick by how much personalization, coaching and accountability you want. Books and self-paced courses give you a method to apply yourself. Apps track sleep and suggest timing. Where the self-directed options fall short

Books, courses and apps all hand the hardest part back to you. They describe a method for an age band, and you decide what to do when your baby does something the method did not describe. That usually happens on the second or third night, at 2 a.m., when you are least able to judge it. Most families who come to Actually Sleeping have already tried one or more of these. What was missing was not information. It was a plan that fit their baby, coaching that prepared them for those moments, and someone to adjust it every day.

What only 1:1 coaching gives you

  • A plan written for your baby, from age, feeding, temperament, how your baby falls asleep now and your family’s schedule.
  • Someone on the first night, when plans most often wobble.
  • Daily adjustment, so a detail that is not working is changed the next day instead of after a week of guessing.
  • Help with complicating factors: reflux under treatment, prematurity, twins, room sharing, a toddler out of the crib, daycare naps.
  • Accountability for the result. With a written guarantee, the outcome is the coach’s responsibility as well as yours.

Where overnight newborn care fits

A night nurse or overnight newborn carer gives you rest in the first weeks, which can be invaluable after a difficult birth or with multiples. It does not change how your baby falls asleep. Many families use overnight care early and add a sleep consultant for foundations or for changing sleep later. See consultant vs night nurse vs doula.

The evidence behind every category

The behavioral methods behind all of these are well studied. A review of 52 studies for the American Academy of Sleep Medicine found 94% reported behavioral interventions effective. The difference between formats is how reliably a family gets the method right for their baby and stays consistent long enough for it to work.

Frequently asked

Is a sleep course as good as a sleep consultant?

The underlying methods are the same. A course leaves fitting the method to your baby and adjusting it night by night to you. A 1:1 consultant does that with you, daily, and the best ones guarantee the result in writing.

Do baby sleep apps work?

They can help you see patterns, because logging shows what is really happening. Tracking alone does not change how your baby falls asleep. A plan written for your baby, with someone reading your log daily, is what turns the data into change.

Can I start with a course and hire a consultant later?

Many families who come to Actually Sleeping did exactly that. Bring your notes on what you tried and for how many nights. It shows Dr. Sam where the plan broke down and makes the new plan sharper.
