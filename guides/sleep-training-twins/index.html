How do you sleep train twins?

The same way as one baby, on the same schedule, in the same room, with the expectation that one will wake the other less often than you fear. Synchronize feeds and naps from the start, put both down awake at the same time, respond to each by the plan rather than rushing to prevent noise, and accept that the first week is louder. Most twin families see both babies sleeping through within the same two weeks.

Principles that matter more with twins

  • Same schedule, always. If one wakes to feed, feed both (or wake the other within 15–20 minutes). Staggered twins mean no sleep for anyone.
  • Same room. Twins habituate to each other’s noise faster than you expect. Separating them for training usually just postpones the problem. White noise helps.
  • Do not referee. Rushing in to keep one from waking the other teaches both that crying brings you fast. Follow the plan for each baby individually.
  • Treat them as individuals within the plan. One may be a faster learner; the plan accounts for temperament. Do not hold the easier sleeper to the harder one’s pace.
  • Two adults, one method. Decide in advance who handles which baby and that both respond identically.

Premature twins

Many twins arrive early. Use corrected age for readiness, get the pediatrician’s sign-off on night feeds for each baby separately, and expect that weight gain, not the calendar, decides when night feeds go.

Why coaching helps disproportionately for twins

There is twice as much data every night and half as much parental capacity to interpret it. A coach reading both babies’ logs each morning and telling you which adjustment to make is the difference between a hard week and a hard season.

Frequently asked

Will one twin wake the other?

Occasionally in the first days, then rarely. Babies habituate to a roommate quickly; adults are usually the ones who cannot sleep through it.

Should twins sleep in the same crib?

No. The AAP recommends a separate sleep surface for each baby.

Can twins be on different schedules?

They can, but it doubles the work and is rarely sustainable. Synchronize feeds and naps as early as possible.
