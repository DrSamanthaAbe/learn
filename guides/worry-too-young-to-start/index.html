Why parents think it is too early

Young babies feel fragile, and much of what parents hear about sleep training assumes an older baby. So it is natural to think you should wait, even when nights are already hard.

Parents in this video felt the same way. Several started anyway, with babies of a few weeks or months, and describe being surprised by how well it went.
