Why nursing moms worry

For many breastfeeding parents, nursing is how everyone gets back to sleep fastest. It is close and quiet and it works, until the baby needs it every hour or two. It is natural to fear that changing sleep means giving up nursing, or giving up something that matters to you and your baby.
