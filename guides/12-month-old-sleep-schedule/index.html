What is a good sleep schedule for a 12 month old?

From 12 to 14 months most toddlers still do best on 2 naps, with wake windows of 3–4.5 hours as a common rule of thumb. The AASM recommends 11–14 hours of sleep in 24 for ages 1–2, naps included. Bedtime usually falls between 7 and 8 p.m., with 11–12 hours overnight and 2–2.5 hours of naps.

Sample day for a 12–14 month old (2 naps)

Treat every time below as a starting point. Shift the whole day to match your baby’s actual wake-up time, then adjust one thing at a time in 15-minute steps and hold it for three days before judging.

What is changing developmentally

Walking, or very nearly. First words, pointing and strong opinions. Around the first birthday many families also change milk and move from bottles to cups; make those changes on your pediatrician’s advice, and keep milk at the start of the bedtime routine rather than as the last step. Daily sleep need drops slightly, which is why a schedule that worked at 10 months can suddenly produce nap refusal or early waking.

Common problems at this age

  • Nap strikes and new night waking: see the 12-month sleep regression.
  • Dropping to one nap too soon: in practice many toddlers make the 2-to-1 move between 14 and 18 months. Signs it is real: a nap refused for 10–14 days, or bedtime pushed past 8:30 p.m. See nap transitions.
  • Early waking: often too much daytime sleep for the new, lower total. See early morning waking.
  • Bottle or nursing to sleep: see the feed-to-sleep association.
  • Bedding: the AAP’s safe sleep recommendations, including keeping soft and loose bedding out of the sleep space, cover babies up to 1 year. After 12 months a wearable blanket is still the simplest choice, since most toddlers cannot keep a blanket on.

Is this age right for sleep training?

Yes. A 12–14 month old is fully able to learn independent sleep, and formal sleep training has been appropriate since about 4 months. Toddlers protest with more stamina and more skill than babies, so consistency between caregivers matters most. Frequently asked

Should a 12 month old be on one nap?

Usually not. In practice many are not ready until around 14–18 months. A 12 month old pushed to one nap often starts waking at night or before 6 a.m.

How much should a 12 month old sleep?

The AASM recommends 11–14 hours per 24 hours for ages 1–2 years, including naps. A typical split is 11–12 hours at night and 2–2.5 hours of naps.

Can my 1 year old have a blanket or pillow?

The AAP’s guidance keeps soft bedding out of the crib for at least the first year. After that, ask your pediatrician; a wearable blanket remains the easy option and pillows are generally left until later in toddlerhood.

What time should a 12 month old go to bed?

Between 7 and 8 p.m., about 3.5–4 hours after the second nap ends.
