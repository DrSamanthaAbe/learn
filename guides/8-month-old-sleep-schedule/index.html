What is a good sleep schedule for an 8 month old?

At 8 months most babies take 2 naps and, as a rule of thumb, stay awake 2.5–3.5 hours at a time. The AASM recommends 12–16 hours of sleep in 24, naps included. Bedtime usually falls between 7 and 7:45 p.m., with 11–12 hours overnight.

Rows marked AASM are the American Academy of Sleep Medicine recommendation, which the AAP supports. Rows marked Galland 2012 come from a systematic review of observational studies. The other rows are common practice ranges, not research findings. They describe an average baby; healthy babies sit on both sides of every one of them.

What is changing developmentally

A burst of motor skills: crawling, pulling to stand, sometimes cruising. The AAP describes stranger anxiety as one of the first emotional milestones; separation anxiety usually peaks between 10 and 18 months, and a baby may wake searching for a parent in the night. These changes often arrive together.

Why the average 8-month schedule often does not fit

  • Sudden night waking or nap refusal: see the 8-to-10-month sleep regression.
  • Standing in the crib and unable to get down: a new skill that turns a short waking into a long one.
  • Crying at separation: the schedule can be perfect and bedtime still falls apart. See separation anxiety at bedtime.
  • New habits from a rough patch: extra rocking or feeding to get through a few hard weeks easily becomes the new requirement. See the feed-to-sleep association.

Safe sleep at 8 months

  • The crib mattress should be at its lowest setting before your baby can stand (AAP, MedlinePlus).
  • Back to sleep to start, nothing loose in the crib, until the first birthday.

Frequently asked

Does an 8 month old still need 3 naps?

Rarely. Studies found babies 6 to 11 months averaged about 2 naps a day, and most 8 month olds are on 2 as a common pattern. If your baby still needs a third, a custom plan works with that rather than forcing a chart.

How long does the 8-month sleep regression last?

There is no research-defined length. It tends to last longer when new sleep habits are introduced to get through it. Keeping your response consistent through it is exactly what Actually Sleeping supports with daily 1:1 check-ins.

What time should an 8 month old go to bed?

As a common rule of thumb, between 7 and 7:45 p.m. The best time for your baby depends on naps and how long they take to fall asleep, so it is set in their plan.

Should I drop night feeds at 8 months?

Some 8 month olds no longer need one and some still do. How night feeds are handled depends on your baby’s age and is set in your custom plan.
