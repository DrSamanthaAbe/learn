What is a good sleep schedule for an 8 month old?

At 8 months most babies take 2 naps and, as a rule of thumb, stay awake 2.5–3.5 hours at a time. The AASM recommends 12–16 hours of sleep in 24, naps included. Bedtime usually falls between 7 and 7:45 p.m., with 11–12 hours overnight.

Sample day for an 8 month old

Treat every time below as a starting point. Shift the whole day to match your baby’s actual wake-up time, then adjust one thing at a time in 15-minute steps and hold it for three days before judging.

The windows here are 2.75, 3.25 and 3.5 hours. If your baby takes a long time to fall asleep, add 15 minutes to the window before that sleep; if they melt down before it, take 15 away.

What is changing developmentally

A burst of motor skills: crawling, pulling to stand, sometimes cruising. Separation anxiety commonly starts between 8 and 12 months, and your baby may cry when you leave the room at bedtime even if they never did before. Language comprehension is growing fast. These changes often arrive together and can disrupt sleep for a few weeks.

Common problems at this age

  • Sudden night waking or nap refusal: see the 8-to-10-month sleep regression.
  • Standing in the crib and unable to get down: practice squatting down from standing many times a day. The mattress should be at its lowest setting before your baby can stand.
  • Crying at separation: play peekaboo and short leave-and-return games in daylight, keep the bedtime routine identical, and say a brief, confident goodnight.
  • New habits from the regression: extra rocking or feeding for two weeks easily becomes the new requirement. See the feed-to-sleep association.

Is this age right for sleep training?

Yes. Formal sleep training is appropriate at 8 months, and a consistent response matters more now because babies this age learn patterns quickly, in both directions. Separation anxiety is a reason to choose a method with a parent present and responsive, which is how Actually Sleeping works: not cry it out, not wait it out. Confirm night-feed needs with your pediatrician. See the best age to sleep train.

Frequently asked

Does an 8 month old still need 3 naps?

Almost never. By 8 months most babies do best on 2 naps with windows of about 2.5–3.5 hours.

How long does the 8-month sleep regression last?

There is no research-defined length. In practice it often passes within a few weeks when the response stays consistent, and lasts longer when new sleep habits are introduced to get through it.

What time should an 8 month old go to bed?

Between 7 and 7:45 p.m., about 3–3.5 hours after the second nap ends.

Should I drop night feeds at 8 months?

That is your pediatrician’s call, based on growth and daytime intake. Many 8 month olds no longer need one, and some still do.
