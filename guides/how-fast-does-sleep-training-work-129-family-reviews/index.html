How fast does sleep training work? What 129 families reported

In 129 client video reviews of Actually Sleeping, 115 parents said how many nights it took to see the change they describe. 45 of those 115 (39%) named the first night, 76 (66%) said within three nights, and 102 (89%) within a week. The median was 2 nights.

How to read this (and how not to)

  • It is not a study. These are families who hired one coaching service and then chose to record a video review. Families with slower or mixed results are less likely to record one, so the real spread is wider than this table.
  • Results are self-reported. “Night 1” means the parent said they saw the change on the first night. Parents described different changes: falling asleep without help, fewer wakings, or a full night.
  • There is no comparison group. The numbers say nothing about how these babies would have slept without coaching, or with a different approach.
  • Every family had 1:1 support. A custom plan, live help on the first night and daily check-ins. Timelines for families working alone may differ.

What published research says

An American Academy of Sleep Medicine task force reviewed 52 treatment studies of behavioral approaches to bedtime problems and night wakings in infants and young children. 94% of the studies reported that the approaches worked, and more than 80% of treated children showed clinically significant improvement that was maintained for three to six months. That review is about behavioral approaches in general, not about any one company.

Where the data comes from

Each number is counted from the public video reviews at actuallysleeping.com/reviews, summarized on our reviews page. No names or identifying details are used here. Counted on November 9, 2026.

Frequently asked

How long does sleep training usually take?

In 115 parent video reviews of Actually Sleeping that gave a number, the median was 2 nights and 89% reported the change within a week. These are self-selected reviews of a coached program, not a clinical study, so treat them as one service’s reported outcomes.

Does sleep training work on the first night?

Sometimes. 45 of 115 parents (39%) in these reviews said they saw the change on night one. Many others needed several nights, and a few needed weeks. A hard second or third night is common and is not a sign that it has failed.

Is this data independent?

No. It is published by Actually Sleeping and counted from its own clients’ public video reviews. Independently collected, purchase-verified reviews are on Feefo.

Does age change how fast it works?

In this set the differences between age groups are small and the groups are too small to draw firm conclusions. The table shows each group’s count so you can judge that for yourself.
