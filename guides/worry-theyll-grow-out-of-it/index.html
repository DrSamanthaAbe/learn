Why parents wait for it to pass

Parents hear it everywhere: it is a phase, a regression, a growth spurt, and your baby will grow out of it. Waiting feels like the sensible, gentle choice.

The parents in this video waited too, some for a year or more. For them, the wake-ups did not fade with time. They changed once each family had a plan.

Why waiting often does not fix it

Night waking is often tied to how a child falls asleep. A child who needs feeding, rocking or a parent in the bed at bedtime tends to need the same help again during the night, and for many families that pattern simply continues as the child gets older.
