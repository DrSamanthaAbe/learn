Why co-sleeping is hard to undo

Many families never plan to co-sleep. It starts on a hard night and becomes the only way anyone sleeps. After months of it, the baby knows no other way to fall asleep, and parents are too tired to try something new.

The parents in this video describe nights of waking every hour, sleeping upright, or one parent moving to the couch. They also describe how it ended.
