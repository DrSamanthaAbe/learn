Why parents fear waking everyone

In a busy house, one baby’s crying does not stay in one room. Parents worry about a toddler woken at midnight, a partner who has to work in the morning, and older children with school the next day. Some keep the baby in their bed simply to keep the peace.

The parents in this video had all of that. They describe a short period of protest and then a house where everyone sleeps.
