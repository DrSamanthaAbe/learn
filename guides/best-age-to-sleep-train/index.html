What is the best age to sleep train a baby?

Between about 4 and 6 months is the sweet spot for most babies: sleep cycles have matured, most babies can go long stretches without feeding if growing well, and habits are still shallow. You can shape sleep from around 10–12 weeks with gentler steps, and you can successfully train at any age after that, including toddlers; it simply takes more consistency the longer a habit has existed.

By age

AgeWhat is realisticNotes
0–10 weeksShaping, not training: day–night differentiation, full feeds, putting down drowsy when it worksNo formal method. Feed on cue. Safe sleep above all.
10–16 weeksBegin separating feeding from falling asleep; consistent bedtime routine; practice putting down awakeExpect partial wins. Night feeds continue.
4–6 monthsFull independent sleep onset; most babies down to 0–1 night feeds with pediatrician agreementThe best window. Fastest results, least protest.
6–12 monthsFully trainable; separation anxiety (8–10 months) and mobility add some noiseStill typically 3–7 nights for nights.
12–24 monthsTrainable in a crib; toddler negotiation beginsConsistency matters more than ever.
2–3 yearsTrainable; bed vs crib is the big variableKeep the crib as long as safely possible; a bed adds freedom to leave.

Why 4–6 months works best

Sleep cycles are now adult-shaped, so what you teach at bedtime transfers to every cycle. Night feeding needs have dropped for most well-growing babies. The baby cannot yet stand, crawl or climb, so protest is vocal rather than physical. And the sleep association is only a few months old.

Corrected age for premature babies

Count from the due date, not the birth date, and get your pediatrician’s sign-off on night feeds. NICU graduates often do very well with coaching once weight gain is solid.

Frequently asked

Is 3 months too early to sleep train?

Too early for formal methods; the right time to start shaping habits gently. Full training usually waits until about 4 months.

Is it too late to sleep train a 2 year old?

No. Toddlers respond quickly to clear, consistent boundaries. It is harder in a bed than a crib.

Can I sleep train during a regression?

Usually yes; the 4-month change is permanent, so there is nothing to wait out. For illness, pause and resume the same plan.
