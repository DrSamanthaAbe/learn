What is the best age to sleep train a baby?

Between about 4 and 6 months is the sweet spot for most babies: sleep cycles have matured and habits are still shallow. Sleep foundations can start from the newborn weeks without restricting feeds, and you can successfully change sleep at any age after that, including toddlers; it simply takes more consistency the longer a habit has existed.

Why 4 to 6 months works so well

By this age the body clock has matured, so what changes at bedtime carries through to every sleep cycle. The baby cannot yet stand, crawl or climb, so protest is vocal rather than physical. And the sleep association is only a few months old.

That said, the best age is usually the one you are in. Actually Sleeping works with babies and toddlers from newborn on, and Dr. Sam writes each plan for the age and stage in front of her, not for an ideal one. Families who started at 4–6 months describe it in the 4–6 month video reviews.

What changes with age

  • Newborn weeks: foundations, not training. Day and night cues, full feeds, safe sleep above all.
  • Second half of the first year: mobility and separation anxiety add noise, and the nap schedule shifts.
  • Toddlers: negotiation, stalling and, in a bed, the freedom to leave. Consistency matters more than ever.

None of these is a reason to wait. Each just changes what the plan has to account for, which is why a one-size-fits-all method so often stalls.

Why later starts take more consistency

A habit that has existed for a year has been repeated hundreds of times. A toddler also understands far more than a baby: they remember what worked last week, they can call for a specific parent, and they can climb. None of that makes change impossible. It means the response has to be clear and identical from every adult, every night, which is hard to hold on your own at 2 a.m. That is exactly where bootcamp-style coaching before the first night and daily check-ins earn their place.

Starting earlier than 4 months

Starting young is the gentlest route, because there is no habit to undo. The practice works with families from the newborn stage: the early work is foundations, and the 14-day guarantee clock starts at the baby’s 10-week birthday. Corrected age for premature babies

For a baby born early, many families use corrected age (age from the due date) when reading age-based guidance. Check feeding and growth questions with your pediatrician.
