When is the right time to hire a baby sleep consultant?

There are two good moments. In the newborn weeks, for foundations: safe sleep, feeding on cue, a simple routine and realistic expectations, with no crying-based training and no stretching of feeds. And later, as soon as sleep has become a problem that is not passing on its own. You do not need to wait until you are exhausted.

Newborn: foundations, not training

A newborn should not be sleep trained. That does not mean nothing can be done. The newborn weeks are when habits form, and a foundations plan shapes them gently. It covers:

  • Safe sleep from night one. Back to sleep, a firm flat surface, nothing soft or weighted in the crib or bassinet, and room sharing without bed sharing, as the AAP recommends.
  • Feeding on cue, day and night, with your pediatrician guiding growth.
  • Day and night cues, and a short, repeatable wind-down that becomes your baby’s signal for sleep.
  • Realistic expectations for what newborn sleep looks like, so you can tell normal from a problem.

Research supports this kind of early guidance. A review of 52 studies for the American Academy of Sleep Medicine found its strongest support for preventive parent education, alongside unmodified extinction. Later: when a problem has settled in

Good signs it is time:

  • Sleep has been hard for more than two weeks, and it is not explained by illness, travel or a new tooth.
  • Your baby wakes at about the same times every night and needs the same help each time.
  • You have tried things and sleep is still broken.
  • Sleep loss is affecting your health, work, relationship or mood.
  • A transition is coming: return to work, daycare, a new sibling, a move.

Picking the start date

You can book the consultation any time. When it comes to night one, a few things affect the date:

  • Illness. Start once your baby is well; see sleep after illness.
  • Medical questions. If your pediatrician is looking into weight gain, breathing or reflux, start once you have the all-clear.
  • Travel. Choose a stretch of about two weeks at home without travel, visitors or big schedule changes.
  • Support at home. It helps when both parents, or a supportive second adult, can be around for the first few nights.

Is it ever too late?

No. Older babies and toddlers respond well to behavioral plans; they tend to have more established habits and more opinions. Actually Sleeping works with children up to 3 years. See sleep consultants for toddlers.

Hiring help is not a sign you failed

Parents often wait until they are exhausted because asking for help feels like giving up. It is not. Most families who get stuck have done many things right and are missing one or two details, or need someone to hold the line with them for a couple of weeks. Getting help earlier means fewer broken nights for everyone. For age-specific context, see the best age to sleep train.

Frequently asked

Can a sleep consultant help with a newborn?

Yes, with foundations: safe sleep, feeding on cue, day and night cues and a simple routine. That is guidance, not sleep training. A consultant should never suggest leaving a newborn to cry or stretching feeds. Actually Sleeping works with families from newborn.

Should I wait until the 4 month regression to hire help?

No need to wait. Starting foundations earlier can make that period smoother, and if sleep has already changed for the worse and stayed that way, getting help now saves weeks of broken nights. The guarantee clock starts at your baby’s 10-week birthday.

Is it better to hire a sleep consultant before going back to work?

Often, yes. Plan for about two weeks at home before your start date so new habits settle before mornings get rushed and daycare naps begin. Book the consultation early so Dr. Sam can plan the timing with you.

Should I hire a consultant while my baby is teething or sick?

You can book the consultation any time, but night one should wait until your baby is well. Illness is a reason to pause. Your plan starts when your baby is healthy and you have two calm weeks ahead.
