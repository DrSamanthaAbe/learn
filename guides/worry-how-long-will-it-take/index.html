Frequently asked

When do families see the first change?

In the video reviews, many families describe a change in the first few nights, and some on night one. Others took longer, up to about ten days. The written guarantee covers 8–12 hours within 14 days of consistent, correct implementation.

Do naps take longer than nights?

Often, yes. Night sleep usually improves first, and naps can take longer to settle. That is why Actually Sleeping coaches naps until they are solid rather than stopping when nights are fixed, with daily 1:1 check-ins throughout.

When does the guarantee clock start for a young baby?

The 14-day clock starts at the baby’s 10-week birthday. Actually Sleeping works with babies from newborn on, and for the youngest babies the early work focuses on foundations. Night feeds are never forced out, and feeding decisions belong to your pediatrician.
