What is a good sleep schedule for an 18 month old?

Between 15 and 23 months most toddlers settle on one midday nap of 1.5–3 hours. As a rule of thumb, wake windows are about 4.5–5.5 hours just after the switch and 5–6 hours from 18 months. The AASM recommends 11–14 hours of sleep in 24, and bedtime usually falls between 7 and 8 p.m.

Sample day for an 18 month old (1 nap)

Treat every time below as a starting point. Shift the whole day to match your baby’s actual wake-up time, then adjust one thing at a time in 15-minute steps and hold it for three days before judging.

Newly on one nap at 15–16 months? Start the nap around 11:30 a.m. and use a 6:30–7 p.m. bedtime, then push the nap later by 15 minutes every few days until it starts between 12 and 12:30 p.m.

What is changing developmentally

The 2-to-1 nap transition, which in practice many toddlers complete between 14 and 18 months. A language burst, and with it the word “no.” A strong drive for independence alongside separation anxiety, which often peaks between 10 and 18 months before easing in the second half of the second year. First molars (about 13–19 months) and canines (about 16–23 months) come through in this window. Toddlers also begin to understand rules and to test whether they hold.

Common problems at this age

  • A messy nap transition: some days need two naps and some one for 2–4 weeks. See nap transitions.
  • Bedtime battles and night waking around 18 months: see the 18-month sleep regression.
  • Short single nap: a nap under an hour usually means it started too early or too late. See short naps.
  • Early waking: see early morning waking.
  • Climbing: keep the mattress at its lowest setting and use a sleep sack. Stay in the crib as long as it is safe; moving to a bed this young usually adds problems.

Is this age right for sleep training?

Yes. It is not too late, and it is not a different process in principle: a predictable routine, a clear and calm response, and the same answer every night. What changes is that toddlers negotiate, so the plan needs firm, kind limits and words your child understands. Frequently asked

When should my toddler drop to one nap?

In practice, many between 14 and 18 months. Look for 10–14 days of a refused nap or a bedtime pushed past 8:30 p.m., not one bad day.

How long should an 18 month old nap?

Usually 1.5–3 hours in a single nap. Wake your toddler by about 3 p.m. to protect bedtime.

What time should an 18 month old go to bed?

Between 7 and 8 p.m., about 5 hours after the nap ends. Use an earlier bedtime during the nap transition.

How long does the 18-month sleep regression last?

There is no research-defined length. In practice it often passes within a few weeks when limits and routine stay consistent.
