What is a good sleep schedule for a 5 month old?

At 5 months most babies take 3 naps, stay awake 2–2.5 hours at a time, and need 12–16 hours of sleep in 24. Bedtime usually falls between 7 and 7:45 p.m., with 11–12 hours overnight and often 0–2 night feeds.

Sample day for a 5 month old

Treat every time below as a starting point. Shift the whole day to match your baby’s actual wake-up time, then adjust one thing at a time in 15-minute steps and hold it for three days before judging.

TimeWhat happens
7:00 a.m.Wake, feed, daylight
9:00 a.m.Nap 1 (aim for 1–1.5 hours)
10:30 a.m.Wake, feed, floor play
12:45 p.m.Nap 2 (aim for 1–1.5 hours)
2:15 p.m.Wake, feed
4:30 p.m.Nap 3, a 30-minute catnap
5:00 p.m.Wake, feed
6:45 p.m.Bath, feed, book, song
7:15 p.m.Into the crib awake, on the back
OvernightOften 0–2 feeds

If naps are still short, keep a fourth catnap for now or bring bedtime forward to as early as 6:30 p.m. An early bedtime does not cause early waking; an overtired bedtime often does.

Numbers at a glance

MeasureTypical range
Total sleep per 24 hours12–16 hours (AASM, ages 4–12 months)
Night sleep11–12 hours
Naps3, totalling about 3–4 hours
Wake windows2–2.5 hours; shortest first, longest before bed
Night feedsCommonly 0–2 a night. Whether to keep, reduce or drop night feeds is your pediatrician’s decision.

What is changing developmentally

The new sleep cycles from the 4-month change have settled, and naps become capable of lengthening past one cycle, though many babies need to learn how. Rolling in both directions, grabbing feet and early sitting practice often show up in the crib at 2 a.m. Daytime feeds get more efficient and more distracted, which can shift calories toward the night if feeds are rushed.

Common problems at this age

  • Naps stuck at 30–45 minutes: at 5 months this is the most common complaint. Check the window first, then how your baby falls asleep. See why naps are only 30 minutes.
  • The regression never ended: if frequent waking has lasted more than about 6 weeks, it is a pattern rather than a phase. See regression or sleep problem.
  • Early waking: usually a too-late bedtime, a too-long last window, or light in the room. See early morning waking.
  • Rolling onto the tummy: always start on the back in an empty crib with no positioners. Give plenty of floor time to practice in the day.

Is this age right for sleep training?

Yes, 5 months is a very workable age for most healthy babies: old enough for formal sleep training, young enough that habits are not deeply set. Confirm with your pediatrician that growth is on track and ask how many night feeds make sense. Frequently asked

Can a 5 month old be on 2 naps?

Rarely. Most 5 month olds cannot comfortably stay awake the 2.5–3.5 hours a two-nap day requires. The move to 2 naps usually happens between 6 and 8 months.

How long should a 5 month old nap?

About 3–4 hours in total: ideally two naps of 1–1.5 hours and one catnap of 30–45 minutes.

What time should a 5 month old go to bed?

Between 7 and 7:45 p.m. for most, 2–2.5 hours after the last nap ends. Use 6:30 p.m. on days with poor naps.
