What is a good sleep schedule for a 7 month old?

At 7 months most babies are on 2 naps or finishing the move from 3. Wake windows of about 2.5–3.25 hours are a common rule of thumb, the AASM recommends 12–16 hours of sleep in 24, and bedtime usually falls between 6:30 and 7:30 p.m. with 11–12 hours overnight.

Sample day for a 7 month old (2 naps)

Treat every time below as a starting point. Shift the whole day to match your baby’s actual wake-up time, then adjust one thing at a time in 15-minute steps and hold it for three days before judging.

Still on 3 naps? Keep windows near 2.25–2.75 hours with a catnap ending by 5:30 p.m. and bedtime around 7:30–8 p.m. Expect a week or two of mixed days during the switch.

What is changing developmentally

Sitting steadily, starting to scoot or crawl, and babbling in strings. Your baby is beginning to understand that people and objects still exist out of sight, which is the root of the separation anxiety that commonly starts between 8 and 12 months. Solids become real meals, and the third nap disappears for most babies.

Common problems at this age

  • The 3-to-2 transition: overtired late afternoons and a rough week of bedtimes. Use a bedtime as early as 6 p.m. as the shock absorber. See nap transitions.
  • Early waking after dropping the nap: usually a last window stretched too far. See early morning waking.
  • Still waking every 2–3 hours: the cause is usually the same as at 6 months; see waking every 2 hours.
  • Practicing skills in the crib: sitting up and getting stuck. Practice getting back down during the day; at night, keep responses brief and boring.

Is this age right for sleep training?

Yes. A 7 month old is well within the age for formal sleep training, and doing it before separation anxiety builds (it usually peaks between 10 and 18 months) is often easier. Frequently asked

Is 7 months too early for 2 naps?

No. In practice many babies move to 2 naps between 6 and 8 months. The test is whether your baby can handle windows of about 2.5–3 hours without falling apart.

How long should a 7 month old’s naps be?

On 2 naps, about 1–1.5 hours each, for 2.5–3 hours in total.

What time should a 7 month old go to bed?

Between 6:30 and 7:30 p.m. on a two-nap day. Go earlier while the third nap is being dropped.
