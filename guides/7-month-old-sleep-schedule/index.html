What is a good sleep schedule for a 7 month old?

At 7 months most babies are on 2 naps or finishing the move from 3. Wake windows of about 2.5–3.25 hours are a common rule of thumb, the AASM recommends 12–16 hours of sleep in 24, and bedtime usually falls between 6:30 and 7:30 p.m. with 11–12 hours overnight.

What is typical at 7 months

MeasureTypical range
Total sleep per 24 hours12–16 hours (AASM, ages 4–12 months)
What studies of healthy babies foundBabies 6–11 months averaged 2.2 naps a day; night wakings at 7–11 months averaged 1.1, slightly more than at 3–6 months (Galland 2012)
Night sleep (common practice range)11–12 hours
Naps (common practice range)2–3, totalling about 2.5–3.5 hours
Wake windows (rule of thumb, not a research finding)2.5–3.25 hours; shortest first, longest before bed
Night feedsCommonly 0–1 a night. Whether to keep, reduce or drop night feeds is your pediatrician’s decision.

Rows marked AASM are the American Academy of Sleep Medicine recommendation, which the AAP supports. Rows marked Galland 2012 come from a systematic review of observational studies. The other rows are common practice ranges, not research findings. They describe an average baby; healthy babies sit on both sides of every one of them.

What is changing developmentally

Sitting steadily, starting to scoot or crawl, and babbling in strings. Your baby is beginning to understand that people still exist out of sight, which is the root of the separation anxiety the AAP says usually peaks between 10 and 18 months. Solids become real meals, and the third nap disappears for many babies.

Why the average 7-month schedule often does not fit

  • The 3-to-2 transition: overtired late afternoons and a rough patch of bedtimes are common. See nap transitions.
  • Early waking after dropping a nap: see early morning waking.
  • Still waking every 2–3 hours: usually the same cause as at 6 months; see waking every 2 hours.
  • Practising skills in the crib: sitting up and getting stuck turns a short waking into a long one.

The schedule matters, but it sits on top of how your baby falls asleep and how they respond to change. A plan has to account for both.

Safe sleep at 7 months

  • Back to sleep to start, in an empty crib, until the first birthday.
  • Mattress lowered for sitting, and at its lowest setting before your baby can stand (MedlinePlus).

Is this age right for sleep training?

Yes. A 7 month old is well within the age many families start, and starting before separation anxiety peaks is often easier. Frequently asked

Is 7 months too early for 2 naps?

No. Many babies move to 2 naps between 6 and 8 months as a common pattern, and studies found babies 6 to 11 months averaged about 2 naps a day. Whether your baby is ready depends on how they cope with longer awake time.

How long should a 7 month old’s naps be?

On 2 naps, a common practice range is about 1–1.5 hours each. Many 7 month olds nap much less, often because of how they fall asleep. Naps are coached until solid in every Actually Sleeping plan.

What time should a 7 month old go to bed?

As a common rule of thumb, between 6:30 and 7:30 p.m. on a two-nap day, and earlier during the transition. The right time for your baby is set in the plan and adjusted during daily check-ins.
