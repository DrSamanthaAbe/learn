What is a good sleep schedule for a 9 month old?

At 9 months babies take 2 naps and, as a rule of thumb, stay awake about 3–3.5 hours at a time. The AASM recommends 12–16 hours of sleep in 24, naps included. Bedtime usually falls between 7 and 7:45 p.m., with 11–12 hours overnight and 2–3 hours of daytime sleep.

Sample day for a 9 month old

Treat every time below as a starting point. Shift the whole day to match your baby’s actual wake-up time, then adjust one thing at a time in 15-minute steps and hold it for three days before judging.

A simple pattern that suits many 9 month olds is 3 hours, then 3.25, then 3.5 hours awake. Cap daytime sleep at about 3 hours so it does not borrow from the night.

What is changing developmentally

Pulling to stand and cruising along furniture, pincer grasp, waving, and understanding simple words like “no” and “bye-bye.” Separation anxiety is often building toward its peak, which usually falls between 10 and 18 months. Three solid meals are established for most babies, which changes the feeding picture overnight. Your baby also now remembers and anticipates routines, which makes a consistent bedtime sequence especially effective.

Common problems at this age

  • Regression in full swing: standing, crying at the door, fighting the second nap. See the 8-to-10-month sleep regression.
  • Early waking: at 9 months often a first nap that is too early or too long, which turns the early wake-up into a habit. See early morning waking.
  • Second nap refused: lengthen the middle window by 15 minutes before assuming the nap is going. Babies this age are not ready for one nap; see nap transitions.
  • Naps only on a parent: see how to stop contact napping.

Is this age right for sleep training?

Yes. Nine months is well past the roughly 4-month starting point for formal sleep training, and it is not too late: older babies often learn in fewer nights because they recognize patterns quickly. Expect more protest about standing and separation than a younger baby would show, and plan your response in advance. Frequently asked

How many naps does a 9 month old need?

Two. Total daytime sleep is usually 2–3 hours.

How long should a 9 month old be awake before bed?

About 3.25–3.5 hours after the second nap ends. Much longer tends to cause night waking and early rising.

Why is my 9 month old suddenly waking at night?

Most often the 8-to-10-month regression: new motor skills plus separation anxiety. Keep the schedule and your response steady; in practice it often passes within a few weeks.
