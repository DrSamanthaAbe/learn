When do babies drop naps, and how do I handle the 3-to-2 and 2-to-1 transitions?

Most babies go from 3 naps to 2 between 6 and 8 months, and from 2 naps to 1 between 14 and 18 months. The signs are consistent: the last nap is refused or pushes bedtime late for two weeks or more. Handle it by lengthening wake windows gradually and moving bedtime earlier for a few weeks, not by dropping the nap abruptly on one bad day.

Signs a transition is real (versus a bad week)

  • The last nap is consistently refused or takes 30+ minutes to happen, for 10–14 days.
  • Naps are fine but bedtime is being pushed past 8:30 p.m. to fit them.
  • Early morning waking appears with no other change.
  • One bad day, an illness or travel is not a transition. Hold the schedule and wait.

3 to 2 naps (about 6–8 months)

  1. Stretch the first two wake windows by 15 minutes every few days until the day holds two naps of 1–2 hours.
  2. Move bedtime earlier (even 6 p.m.) on days the second nap ends early. Early bedtime is the shock absorber.
  3. Expect 1–2 weeks of some days with three naps and some with two.

2 to 1 nap (about 14–18 months)

  1. Push the morning nap later by 15–30 minutes every few days, aiming for a single nap starting around 12–12:30.
  2. Protect bedtime: move it to 6–6:30 p.m. during the transition.
  3. Offer a quiet-time or a short late catnap if the day falls apart, then resume the push.
  4. Give it 2–4 weeks. This is the longest transition.

The common mistake

Dropping the second nap at 12 months because of a few refusals. Twelve-month-olds are usually in a regression, not a transition; hold two naps and the refusals pass. A premature 2-to-1 transition produces weeks of night waking and early rising.

Frequently asked

My 12 month old refuses the second nap. Is it time for one nap?

Usually not. Twelve months is a regression age; most toddlers still need two naps until 14–18 months. Hold the schedule for two weeks before deciding.

How long should the single nap be?

Typically 2–3 hours once established, usually starting around midday.

When do toddlers stop napping entirely?

Most between 3 and 5 years. Keep a quiet rest time after the nap goes.
