Is melatonin safe for babies and toddlers?

No for babies, and not recommended for toddlers without a pediatrician’s direction. Melatonin is a hormone, it is sold as a supplement the FDA does not regulate for this use, product strength can differ from the label, and infants’ own melatonin rhythm is still developing. A baby who will not sleep usually has a behavioral or schedule cause that melatonin does not fix.

Why melatonin is not recommended for babies

  • It is a hormone, not a sleeping pill. Melatonin signals time of day to the body. The AAP is direct about it: melatonin is not a sleeping pill. A baby’s own melatonin rhythm is still developing in the first months.
  • Supplements are not drugs. The AAP notes that melatonin sold as a dietary supplement is not regulated by the FDA for this use, and that some products have been found to contain much more melatonin than the label lists.
  • Accidental ingestions are rising. The AAP reports more than 260,000 reports of child melatonin poisonings from 2012 to 2021, and two children died. Gummies look like candy. Keep any melatonin in the house where a toddler cannot reach or see it.
  • It treats the wrong problem. Babies who wake every two hours are usually not short on melatonin. They have a sleep association, a schedule mismatch, or both.

When a pediatrician might consider it

For some older children with specific medical or developmental conditions, a pediatrician or sleep specialist may decide melatonin is appropriate, with a chosen product and timing. The AAP says melatonin should only be used after a discussion with your pediatrician. That is a medical decision made for one child, not a shortcut for ordinary night waking.

Medicine is not the answer to a sleep problem

The AAP calls good sleep habits the best medicine. The hard part is that “habits” means something different for every child. Frequently asked

Can I give my 1 year old melatonin?

Not without your pediatrician’s direction. The AAP says melatonin should only be used after a discussion with your pediatrician and calls good sleep habits the best medicine. Most toddler night waking is behavioral, which is the part a custom sleep plan from Actually Sleeping is designed to change.

Does melatonin have side effects in children?

The AAP lists morning sleepiness, drowsiness and possible nighttime urination as the most common, notes that melatonin may interact with other medicines, and says long-term effects are less well understood. If a child swallows melatonin by accident, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222.

Are ‘natural’ sleep gummies for toddlers safe?

‘Natural’ is a marketing word, not a safety standard. Check the ingredients; many contain melatonin or herbs with no pediatric safety data. The AAP notes some supplements contain far more melatonin than the label says. Ask your pediatrician before giving any of them.
